# Bold · decoration, content and density

**Idea.** Each lens is a different sheet of the same set, drawn for a different reader. Plan,
positions and stage fills never change; the lens brings its own paper, ornaments, accent ink, words
and sheet panel. Accents touch marks, ornaments and evidence text only, never a tile fill.

**General** stays calm: no decoration, six health cells along each fixture's top edge, each capped
with its lens accent. On a switch the cells fly to the tile's lower-right corner and become that
lens's instrument while the other five fade; decoration follows dominance, so lens to lens passes
through the bare base.

| lens | paper and rails | tile | panel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ledger | ledger ruling, "41 customer asks · $3k a month" | value pips, asks bar, ticket notches | plain sentences (standard) |
| Artboard | dot canvas, crop marks | fidelity ladder, rounded corners | one big fact (simple) |
| Terminal | survey grid, dimension strings, PR/AI counts | block progress bar, chamfers | mono key/values (dense) |
| Control | flow chevrons, gauge rulers, live counts | rollout dial, pill | live gauges (dense) |
| Perimeter | hatched fence, fenced rooms, padlocks | review seal, fence by data class, shield | seal, data-class ladder (standard) |
| Inspection | graph paper, checklists | e2e checklist, P1 dots, tag | test boxes, bug counts (standard) |

Each building also gets a lens title block in empty paper under a short wing. Queue cards gain one
line in the lens's voice.

**Limits.** `decor()` reads `window.__bp` for camera and plan (the seam passes neither). Title blocks
need a wing gap (none at `?scale=4` L0). Outlines apply to fixtures at least 120 px wide. A zoomed
lens switch at `?scale=4` sits on the frame-budget edge (p50 16.7 to 33 ms, headless software).
