# S8. End card: tomorrow morning, then the title (2:41-2:50)

Script: [../scenes.md](../scenes.md#s8-end-card-tomorrow-morning-then-the-title-241-250).

## Setup

- **Headset part:** linked, `kettle-live`. Before the take: `BpStandup.Last 30` (the last stand-up 30 hours ago), so the
  stand-up opens with "Welcome back" and the bar and MENU say what came of yesterday. Do this after S5 and S6 are in the
  can (it changes the stand-up's state).
- **Title card:** made in the edit, on black: "Developer's Blueprint" large; under it "Productivity · Hands only"; a third
  line "72 fps on Quest 3S" only if the frame rate was confirmed with a person wearing the headset (the device bench says
  72-73 fps unattended; the claim needs the worn check). No logos, no store badges, no engine name.

## Steps

1. Recorder on. Look at the bar (it shows the outcome line). MENU: the STAND-UP tile says the same. Tap STAND-UP.
2. Glim: "Welcome back. Here is what your approvals became." The sheet: "Welcome back. Since <yesterday's time>: ...".
3. Hold 2 s. Stop.

## Takes to get

- 2 takes. A still of the bar's outcome line for a freeze if the take is short.

## What can go wrong

- No "Welcome back": the last stand-up was not set back far enough, or nothing was approved in 7 days (reset
  `kettle-live`).
- The outcome counts are from the demo data, not from the S5 approval: the caption says "what moved", never "your
  approval was built".

## Post

- 5 s of the headset, fade to black, 4 s title, fade out by 2:50. Music up to the end.
