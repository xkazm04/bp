# S7. Your room (2:35-2:41, optional) or TEXT SIZE

Script: [../scenes.md](../scenes.md#s7-your-room-235-241-headset-optional).

## Setup

- **First, can it be filmed?** Whether the headset's recorder captures passthrough is unconfirmed (dry run). If it does
  not, use the TEXT SIZE insert below.
- **The room:** what passthrough shows is the owner's real room. No person, no screen with readable content, no logo on a
  monitor, keyboard, mug, poster or box, no window to a recognizable street. Clear the desk; a plain notebook and a pen
  are fine.
- **Settings:** the look tuned in the dry run (`bp.Room.Brightness`, `bp.Room.Contrast`, `bp.Room.Shadow`). Glim's self
  light visible against the room (`bp.Lamp.SelfLight`).

## Steps

1. Recorder on, studio showing. Tap STUDIO on the bar. Hold 4 s with the table and the real desk in frame. Stop.
2. Put the room back (YOUR ROOM) after the take: the choice is remembered across launches.

## TEXT SIZE insert (the alternative)

1. MENU > SETTINGS > ACCESSIBILITY by hand (Glim: `page-access`, "Motion, text size, captions, and which hand holds the
   wrist."). Tap 130 %: the console, the wrist and the captions grow together. Known: Glim's caption touches the bar's
   left end at 130 %. Put it back to 100 % after (`bp.TextSize 1`).
2. Caption: "Text at 115 or 130 %, reduced motion, captions, either hand."

## Takes to get

- 2 takes of the room switch; 2 of TEXT SIZE.

## Post

- Real time, 6 s. If the room shows anything identifiable, drop the scene; never blur in post (the rule is about what is
  shown).
