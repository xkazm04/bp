## The biggest remaining gaps, as seen from the target pose

1. **Density.** The concept shows 13 wings packed with furnished rooms. Kettle has 5 wings, so the sheet reads emptier.
   This is the data, not the renderer: VR draws what `/plan` holds.
2. **The lifted far room floats past the far rim.** The lift clamp keeps the footprint over the paper in plan. But a room
   10 cm up at the far edge projects beyond the rim from a low eye.
3. **The lamp agent's read.** The concept's agent is a white, rounded figure with a glowing face. The imported asset is a
   jointed desk lamp, true to its manifest's 45 cm.
4. **The near rim band** and **small count labels**, as in WP3.
